# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want CupBack to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, experience or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient.
- Respect different ideas and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback with care.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Sexual language or imagery
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

In all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@thesithunyein](https://github.com/thesithunyein), privately. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or block people who break this code.
