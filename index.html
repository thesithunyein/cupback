FAQ

Where can I return my cup?

At a booth that participates in your event. Ask pickup staff which booths accept returns.

Which wallet do I need?

Use a Bitcoin Cash wallet that supports WizardConnect, such as Cashonize. Your wallet must use the same network as the event. This site currently uses Chipnet.

What if I lose my receipt?

Open Find receipt on the same browser you used to pay. Recent deposits are saved on that device. You can also reopen a saved receipt link.

How can I tell if I got my refund?

Keep your receipt open. When the refund appears on the network, the receipt updates and links to the transaction. You can also refresh it yourself.

What if I miss the return window?

Open your receipt to check the event’s recovery time. Once recovery opens, you can use the original wallet to reclaim an unspent deposit.

How do I run CupBack at my event?

Open Set up an event, enter the deposit and the wallets that can approve returns, and share the pickup QR with your booth staff.

Deposits and refunds settle on Bitcoin Cash.
