FAQ

Where can I return my cup?

Ask staff which booths take returns. Bring your cup and receipt.

Which wallet do I need?

Use a wallet with WizardConnect, like Cashonize. Set it to Chipnet, the Bitcoin Cash test network.

What if I lose my receipt?

Open Find receipt in the browser you paid with. You can also open a receipt link you saved.

How can I tell if I got my refund?

Keep your receipt open. It will show Refunded when the payment appears. Tap the link to check it.

What if I miss the return window?

Check the recovery time on your receipt. After that time, you can use the same wallet to get back a deposit that hasn’t been refunded.

How do I run CupBack at my event?

Open Set up an event. Add the deposit and staff wallets. Share the pickup QR code.

Deposits and refunds settle on Bitcoin Cash.
